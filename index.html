About

I research armed conflict, counterinsurgency and peacebuilding in South Asia. My doctoral work was an ethnographic study of the unintended consequences of India's surrender and rehabilitation policy for state–society relations in Maoist central India.

Before academia I worked in humanitarian response, civilian protection and monitoring and evaluation, including as a Civilian Protection Officer with Nonviolent Peaceforce in Mindanao, Philippines, and in research on violence in Manipur.
